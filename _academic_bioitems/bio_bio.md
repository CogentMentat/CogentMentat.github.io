I smash data analysis together with text and music to measure cultural innovation.
    
I am a PhD at [NYU Abu Dhabi](https://nyuad.nyu.edu/en/), previously at the [Center for Complex Networks and Systems](http://cnets.indiana.edu/). I have researched language use with respect to social movements, politics, and group formation. Currently I am researching cultural change in the presence of new AI technologies, particularly in amateur music production.

* Previous mentors:
  - [Johan Bollen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=jDmcdsUAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao)
  - [Simon DeDeo](https://scholars.cmu.edu/7415-simon-dedeo)
  - [Doug Fields](https://physics.unm.edu/people/faculty/douglas-fields.html)

I have reviewed for [Cognitive Science](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/15516709), [PLOS One](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/), and [ICWSM](https://www.icwsm.org).

In the past, I helped [smash particles together]({{site.url}}/projects/p1-FVTX/).

I enjoy making music and music videos.
